# NEXARIUM — Revue trimestrielle

**NEXARIUM** is an independent quarterly online review dedicated
to culture, science, research, heritage, the arts, and contemporary
phenomena.

- Website: https://nexarium.nvo987.fr/
- Publisher: NVO987 – Culture Visuelle Moderne et Contemporaine
- Language: French
- Publication frequency: Quarterly
- First listed issue: Volume I, No. 01
- First listed issue period: First quarter of 2026
- Contact: contact@nvo987.fr

## About

NEXARIUM provides an editorial space for exploring knowledge,
exchanging ideas, and examining contemporary subjects through
interdisciplinary perspectives.

The review covers six principal areas:

1. Culture
2. Science
3. Research
4. Heritage
5. Arts
6. Society

Its editorial approach emphasizes contextual analysis,
interdisciplinary research, and the accessible presentation
of knowledge.
